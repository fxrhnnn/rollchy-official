Our Story & Mission

About Rollchy

Rollchy is a snack brand inspired by student campus life, originally operated for around three months in early 2026. The name Rollchy is a fusion of “Roll” (from our signature Ramen Roll) and “chy” (from irresistible crunchiness).

Our Mission

To offer enjoyable, delicious, and affordable snacks to campus students and the surrounding residential community.

Our Vision

To build recognition as a favorite local snack brand known for value, taste, and high customer satisfaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed customer service policies and project simulation guidelines.

What products does Rollchy feature?

Rollchy features three categories: Ramen Roll Original (RM3.90), Homemade Muruku Original (from RM5.00), and the proposed Cheese Popia Crispy (RM8.00).

How can I contact Rollchy?

You can contact us via our WhatsApp direct link (018-224-5848) or through our official Instagram page (@rollchy_).

What are the operating hours?

10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. is the proposed schedule for this academic simulation, not verified current hours.

What payment methods are proposed?

Online bank transfer and cash on pickup may be described as proposed methods for this simulated ordering process.

Are pickup and delivery available?

The website describes proposed pickup (meeting around campus) and local delivery arrangements for the simulation only.

What if an order is incorrect or damaged?

Customers can contact Rollchy directly via WhatsApp so the issue can be reviewed and an appropriate response or replacement discussed.

Can I place a real order through this website?

No. The website is an academic simulation for the DPS30153 E-Commerce project and is not an active online store. No financial transactions are processed.
