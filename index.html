Our Story & Mission

About Rollchy

Rollchy is a snack brand inspired by student campus life, originally operated for around three months in early 2026. The name Rollchy is a fusion of “Roll” (from our signature Ramen Roll) and “chy” (from irresistible crunchiness).

Our Mission

To offer enjoyable, delicious, and affordable snacks to campus students and the surrounding residential community.

Our Vision

To build recognition as a favorite local snack brand known for value, taste, and high customer satisfaction.

Data Transparency & Ethics

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

We value student and visitor privacy. Learn how names, messages, and order inquiry details are protected under strict data minimization guidelines.

DPS30153 Academic Project Simulation Notice

This website has been developed for educational purposes as part of the DPS30153 E-Commerce project at Politeknik Sultan Azlan Shah (PSAS). This draft privacy policy is tailored for an academic simulation and requires comprehensive review before real commercial business application.

1. Minimal Data Collection

We practice strict data minimization. We only receive details voluntarily submitted through our academic feedback form or WhatsApp link (Full Name, Phone Number/Email, and Message). We do not collect credit cards, banking numbers, identity cards, or passwords.

2. Purpose of Information Use

Any collected information is utilized solely to evaluate student project deliverables, test simulated customer support workflows, and respond to project inquiries. Data is never repurposed for marketing promotions, telemarketing, or commercial advertising.

3. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

Your submitted information is treated with strict confidentiality. Rollchy does not sell, rent, trade, or transfer personal data to any external third-party organizations. All information remains restricted to the academic project team.

4. Data Retention & Simulated Orders

All feedback submissions and temporary communication records are kept only for the duration of the semester grading period and permanently deleted thereafter. As this is a simulation, no real monetary transactions are processed.

For questions regarding our academic data practices or to request removal of your feedback, reach out via WhatsApp (018-224-5848) or Instagram (@rollchy_).
